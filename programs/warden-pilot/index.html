Warden pilot partners

Creators Common is preparing an initial partner programme for Warden-enabled services. The proposed entry experience includes Virtual Silk Road (VSR) and DigitalMe by default, subject to technical readiness and valid licensing rights.

Programme proposal: applications are not yet open.

No live licence sale, booking, access grant or service engagement is offered through this page. An approved application destination, privacy notice, pilot terms and authorized contacts must be published before recruitment begins.

Who may participate?

Marketing agencies, consultancies, technology teams and qualified service providers may be considered for a bounded pilot. The independent pilot company contracts with and invoices its customer directly, and provides the agreed service under its own contractual obligations.

Four eligible production conditions

Genesis stack + Commons production

Commons production using authorized Genesis capabilities.

Non-Genesis stack + Commons production

Commons production using independently authorized technology.

Genesis stack + external production

External production with authorized Genesis capabilities.

Non-Genesis stack + external production

External production using independent technology.

Third-party stack, production and distribution participation can be recorded separately under each combination.

Who does what?

Esomoire is the proposed administrator of registries, marketplace listings, booking references and permitted reporting. This does not make it the product owner, distributor, pilot customer, contracting provider, invoice issuer or payment recipient.

Creators Common recognizes eligible creator participation. Warden governs consent and admission; Synnergyze orchestrates admitted work; authorized ARKs execute; River records evidence. The public site does not issue identity or execution authority.

Creator compensation arising from product licences requires documented rights and agreed terms. Optional direct creator services are separately contracted and charged.

Other products

Quantum Arc will have a separate commercial release. Quantum Room is device-dependent and will not launch commercially without its corresponding validated physical device.

What happens next?

Programme owner, rights and public claims must be approved; an official application channel and privacy notice must be named; onboarding, admission and reporting processes must pass review. Any future WhatsApp invitation will direct interested participants to the approved intake channel.
