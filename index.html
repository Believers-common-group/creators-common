Creation must remain connected to truth.

Every creation should preserve its origin, contributors, controlled version, supporting evidence, permitted uses, responsibilities and economic allocation. Creators Common supplies that continuity without pretending to replace legal or regulatory institutions.

01

Identity

Verified creator, contributor, reviewer, operator and institutional identities through portable Creator Passports.

02

Provenance

Traceable lineage from concept and prototype to validation, release, derivatives, improvements and preservation.

03

Evidence

Claims remain linked to tests, designs, decisions, approvals, deviations and immutable historical records.

04

Licensing

Field of use, territory, duration, volume, obligations, restrictions and derivative rights are explicitly governed.

05

Participation

Attribution and economic rights are assigned to the people and institutions that actually contributed value.

06

Preservation

Custodianship, archives, source materials and knowledge-transfer controls protect continuity beyond one project or owner.

Creators remain connected to what they create. Creations remain connected to their evidence. Every authorised use remains connected to responsibility and value distribution.

Two passports. One continuous record.

The Creator Passport identifies the person or institution. The Creation Passport identifies the work. Contribution and Licence Records connect both to verifiable action and authorised use.

PERSON

Creator Passport

Identity, capability, affiliations, contributions, reviewer roles, licences, training and an evidence-backed portfolio.

WORK

Creation Passport

Purpose, technical definition, creators, ownership, evidence, version, maturity, restrictions, economics and lineage.

USE

Licence Record

Permitted parties, territory, duration, field of use, evidence duties, royalties, derivatives and suspension conditions.

From idea to preserved creation.

A controlled maturity ladder prevents an early concept from being presented as a validated product and protects every stage of genuine progress.

C0 · Idea registeredProblem, opportunity and originator recorded.
C1 · Concept definedPurpose, preliminary design and contributors established.
C2 · Prototype createdInitial working embodiment and known limitations captured.
C3 · VerifiedTechnical function demonstrated against defined requirements.
C4 · ValidatedFitness for intended use evaluated and bounded.
C5 · Production-readyControlled specification and release package completed.
C6 · Licensed deploymentApproved parties may manufacture, deploy or use the creation.
C7 · Network creationInteroperable use across affiliated nodes with shared evidence rules.
C8 · Preserved creationCustodian, archive, continuity and knowledge transfer completed.

Discipline-level programmes.

Each guild applies the same constitutional model to the evidence, risks, maturity gates, licences and responsibilities of its field.

First implementation

Molecular Sciences · qPCR Creator Programme

The first controlled programme registers and governs:

  • assays, primers, probes, controls and reference materials;
  • extraction, reverse-transcription and laboratory protocols;
  • thermal, optical, fluidic and automation hardware;
  • analysis software, instrument connectors and evidence systems;
  • scientists, engineers, validation reviewers and laboratory nodes.

Institutional chain

Governance to distribution

Believers Common provides constitutional authority. DigitalMe identifies participants. Synnergyze operates the registry. RiverOS preserves evidence. Warden enforces permissions. EmpireOS issues governed licences. The Virtual Silk Road enables controlled discovery, collaboration and exchange.

No registry entry or ecosystem licence substitutes for a patent, copyright registration, professional qualification, laboratory accreditation, regulated-product authorisation or legally required approval.
