# Esomoire administrative bridge for first Warden pilot — R0.8

**Status:** Draft architecture and synthetic-only implementation. No intake, booking, listing, licence, identity, distribution or real customer transactions.

## Purpose
Demonstrate the Creators Common pilot handoff as **administrative references**, not a commercial transaction in which Esomoire becomes the principal. This implementation is a read-only, deterministic TypeScript function and a non-indexed Next.js static demonstration.

## Source and limits
- `lib/programs/warden-pilot-esomoire.ts`: validates a deliberately synthetic, bounded candidate application and produces linked administrative registry, listing and booking identifiers, all marked non-authoritative.
- `lib/programs/warden-pilot-esomoire-fixture.ts`: artificial company and service example; no real applicant.
- `app/programs/warden-pilot/administration/page.tsx`: public preview **in a draft PR only**; no form, runtime writes, API calls or actual booking.
- `tests/warden-pilot-esomoire.test.ts`: checks default-deny input boundaries, classification and economic separation, no live identity, and reference linkage.
- Previous R0.7 gate register and private-data restrictions remain authoritative for this proposal's deployment status.

## Administrative records
`candidate.id` → `registry.candidateId` → `listing.registryId` → `booking.listingId`

All IDs are marked `EXAMPLE-` for synthetic use. Registry = `PREVIEW_ONLY`, listing = `NOT_PUBLISHED`, booking = `NOT_BOOKABLE`. Nothing in this demonstration constitutes product ownership, authority, service acceptance, booking availability, calendar allocation or customer payment.

## Commercial boundary
1. The pilot partner may **under an explicitly bounded, approved pilot grant** contract directly with its client, deliver the agreed service and issue its own invoice. Neither a partner application nor a listing grants this power.
2. Esomoire can, after separate appointment, administer product and participant registry records, authorized marketplace projections, booking requests and agreed reporting references.
3. Esomoire has **no presumptive access** to customers, invoices, payment providers, bank records, prices, revenue, private contracts or client data. Only specifically authorized administrative metadata is shareable under a separately agreed report and privacy controls.
4. Contributor attribution, remuneration from purchased licences, and optional monthly creator services are distinct agreements. Warden is still pilot-stage and not an available general licence inventory item.
5. After verified product graduation, a rights-authorized business might obtain distribution rights. This is a separate legal appointment and cannot be inferred from Esomoire's bootstrap stewardship.

## Four conditions, independent third-party axis
- G-C: Genesis stack + Commons production
- NG-C: non-Genesis stack + Commons production
- G-E: Genesis stack + external production
- NG-E: non-Genesis stack + external production

A third party can supply stack, production, both or separately licensed distribution. A third-party designation cannot erase the accountable original production source.

## Proposed progression from mock records to production
**A — Current:** Synthetic in-memory preview, no storage or user input, all R0.7 gates HOLD.
**B — Restricted candidate intake:** First approve programme/rights authority, applicant controller, privacy notice, secure application endpoint, reviewer permissions, retention, signed participation terms and authorised Esomoire data-sharing scope. Must keep private applicant data off the public site and GitHub.
**C — Administrative registry:** Implement authenticated, tenant-scoped, durable storage; human decisions; idempotent state transitions; dedupe, revocation and audit; only publish explicit permitted metadata. Esomoire may see assigned records only.
**D — Booking coordination:** Provider-owned availability, opt-in customer initiation, explicit confirmation and cancellation; no implied service authority. A booking is not payment or Warden admission.
**E — Pilot execution:** Independently verify a bounded rights grant, provider/customer agreements, per-task Warden approval, admitted ARK execution and River receipts before live activity.
**F — Graduation:** Independent product readiness/IP/licensing/tax review, deliberate distribution appointments, and customer-visible commercial terms. Not automatic from pilot completion.
