## A. What registration actually means
The applicant is **an independent pilot service company**, not necessarily a Warden author, a product buyer, a reseller, or an Esomoire customer. Its company-level application is an expression of interest; it is not a technology licence, creator copyright registration, Warden permit, customer agreement, booking, or permission to advertise live availability.

### Proposed applicant packet (not an active web form)
1. Legal company name, business identifier and operating jurisdiction (verify before authorization; avoid displaying identifiers publicly).
2. Authorized business contact and proof of representative authority; private secure submission only.
3. The pilot service proposed, target customer segment, jurisdiction, scope and excluded actions.
4. Classification: Genesis or non-Genesis stack × Commons or external production, with named additional third-party roles if applicable.
5. Underlying stack and product licence claims, rights holder, evidence pointers and proposed restrictions.
6. Proposed pilot customer contract, billing entity and invoice issuer (default: **the pilot company**).
7. Minimal intended integration capabilities, credentials handling design, consent mechanism, tenant boundaries and data classes; **never request passwords, tokens or customer datasets in the application**.
8. Service acceptance criteria, escalation policy, reversibility/rollback, incident contacts and monthly creator-service option if separately contracted.
9. Confirmation of willingness to link Esomoire registry, listing and booking identifiers and furnish only **agreed** aggregated/disposition reports; no implied access to invoices, payment flows or private client records.
10. Company agreement to qualification review, independent authority checks and a **separate** written pilot authorization before any action.

## B. Administrative separation
**Believers Common:** public announcement and opt-in recruitment projection; no hidden forms or personal trackers.
**Creators Common:** programme participation, independent contributor attribution and separate licence compensation records.
**Esomoire:** administrative registry, listings, bookings and references to contract/licence/evidence where permitted. Esomoire is **not** the product owner, buyer, licensor by default, distributor, contracting service company, collector or invoice issuer.
**Independent pilot provider:** signs customer contract, bills and delivers its authorized service; has no blanket rights to Warden/Genesis/third-party software.
**Genesis/Warden/Synnergyze/ARKs/River:** existing boundaries for identity, admission, orchestration, execution and verifiable evidence. A listing or booking does not grant execution rights.

## C. Consent and privacy (draft policy, not approved notice)
- No live application intake until an authorized data controller and published contact, purpose-specific privacy notice, retention term, hosting/processor list, rights-handling method and secure application endpoint are approved.
- Data minimization: collect corporate/application eligibility information only; avoid end-customer names, contact lists, screen recordings, payment records, health details, credentials and live customer content.
- Clarify whether/when contact details may be shared with Esomoire solely for agreed administration. Do not presume membership in WhatsApp constitutes consent to data sharing.
- Use explicit and auditable opt-in for WhatsApp communications, independent of pilot acceptance; provide unsubscribe/withdrawal channel.
- Access control: tenant-scoped reviewers, least privilege, no public submissions in GitHub Issues or public repo files, no public applicant directory without separate approval.
- Define correction, withdrawal, rejection, deletion/retention, incident response, subprocessors and dispute routes before intake. Consider India's applicable DPDP obligations with qualified advice.
- Pilot/customer agreements and production deployment data flows require independent assessment; this programme page is not a privacy notice or a substitute for signed consent.
